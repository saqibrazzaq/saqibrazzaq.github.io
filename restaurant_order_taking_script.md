# OUTBACK GRILL & BAR - Phone Order Script

## Scene Setup
- **Restaurant:** Outback Grill & Bar (Australian Cuisine)
- **Setting:** Restaurant phone support during lunch hours (around 11:35 AM)
- **Duration:** ~3-4 minutes
- **Characters:** Sarah (Restaurant Support), Michael (Customer)
- **Context:** Michael is ordering from the office for same-day lunch pickup

---

## SCRIPT

### [Phone rings - Upbeat, professional greeting]

**SARAH (Support):** 
"Hello, thank you for calling Outback Grill & Bar! This is Sarah speaking. How can I help you today?"

**MICHAEL (Customer):** 
"Hi Sarah, I'd like to place a pickup order for lunch today."

**SARAH:** 
"Absolutely! What time were you thinking of picking up?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Ideally around 12:30? I'm at the office and want to grab something quick."

**SARAH:** 
"12:30 works perfectly for us. Can I start by taking your name?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Sure, it's Michael Chen."

**SARAH:** 
"Great, Michael. So 12:30 PM pickup today. Now, have you ordered from us before?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"No, actually this is my first time. A colleague recommended Outback Grill to me, and I really want to try it. What would you recommend?"

**SARAH:** 
"Oh, wonderful! Welcome to Outback Grill & Bar! Our Signature Beef & Guinness Pie is one of our most popular items—it's a classic Australian favorite and absolutely delicious. It comes with your choice of sides: chips, garden salad, or pumpkin and sweet potato mash. Plus a small drink is included."

**MICHAEL:** 
"That sounds great! How quickly can you make it?"

**SARAH:** 
"The pies are made fresh throughout the day. For a 12:30 pickup, you'll have your order ready and waiting for you."

**MICHAEL:** 
"Perfect! I'll take the Beef & Guinness Pie with chips and a Lemonade."

**SARAH:** 
"Wonderful! One Beef & Guinness Pie with chips and Lemonade. That's $18.95. Would you like anything else?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Maybe something to start with? What do you recommend?"

**SARAH:** 
"Our Damper Bread with native pepper butter is really popular—it's warm and delicious. Only $7.95. Or if you'd like something more substantial, we have Crispy Calamari for $12.50."

**MICHAEL:** 
"The Damper Bread sounds good. Is it spicy?"

**SARAH:** 
"The native pepper butter has a nice subtle flavor—not too spicy, but it has a bit of character to it. Most customers really enjoy it."

**MICHAEL:** 
"Alright, I'll take that. Actually, can I add one more order? My partner wants something too. She's vegetarian."

**SARAH:** 
"Of course! We have some great vegetarian options. Our Roasted Vegetable & Halloumi Stack is very popular—it has roasted seasonal vegetables with grilled halloumi cheese and comes with sides and a drink. That's $16.95. Or we have a Beetroot & Goat Cheese Salad for $14.95."

**MICHAEL:** 
"The Halloumi Stack sounds perfect for her. Can that come with the pumpkin mash instead?"

**SARAH:** 
"Absolutely, no problem at all. So let me confirm your order: one Beef & Guinness Pie with chips and Lemonade, one Damper Bread with native pepper butter, and one Roasted Vegetable & Halloumi Stack with pumpkin mash. All for pickup at 12:30 PM today. Is that correct?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Yes, that's perfect!"

**SARAH:** 
"Great! Let me just add that up. One Beef & Guinness Pie combo is $18.95, Damper Bread is $7.95, and the Roasted Vegetable & Halloumi Stack is $16.95. Your total comes to **$43.85**."

**MICHAEL:** 
"Sounds good."

**SARAH:** 
"Perfect. And just to confirm—Michael, can I get an email address for the receipt?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Sure, it's m.chen@email.com."

**SARAH:** 
"Thank you. So to finalize, your order total is $43.85 for pickup at 12:30 PM today. Shall I go ahead and book this for you?"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Yes, let's do it."

**SARAH:** 
"Excellent! Your order has been confirmed and sent to the kitchen. You'll receive a confirmation SMS shortly with your order details and our address. Your pickup time is 12:30 PM today. Thank you for choosing Outback Grill & Bar!"

**MICHAEL:** 
"Thanks, Sarah! I'll see you at 12:30."

**SARAH:** 
"See you then!"

**[Call ends]**

## KEY SCRIPT ELEMENTS COVERED

✓ Same-day pickup order  
✓ Natural, conversational tone  
✓ Gathering customer details (name, email, pickup time)  
✓ Phone number already visible from phone app  
✓ Welcoming first-time customer  
✓ Menu recommendations without overselling  
✓ Answering practical questions about food and preparation  
✓ Accommodating special dietary requirements  
✓ Confirming order step-by-step  
✓ Clear itemization and price breakdown  
✓ Final order summary with pickup time  
✓ Post-order confirmation (SMS notification)  

---

## PERFORMANCE TIPS FOR VIDEO

1. **Sarah (Support):** Speak naturally and professionally, friendly but efficient, not overly enthusiastic
2. **Michael (Customer):** Sound natural, ask practical questions, be conversational
3. **Pacing:** Keep natural conversation pace, no artificial pauses
4. **Background:** 
   - For Sarah: Soft restaurant ambiance in the background
   - For Michael: Subtle office environment
5. **Lower third graphics:** 
   - Display restaurant name and phone number
   - Show pickup time (12:30 PM)
   - Display running order total
6. **Visual elements:**
   - Order details displayed on screen
   - Final confirmation with SMS notification preview
7. **Tone:** Keep it real and casual, like an actual phone call
